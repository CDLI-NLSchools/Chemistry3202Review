# Chemistry 3202 Review

A single-file practice app for Chemistry 3202 (Newfoundland and Labrador curriculum): generated practice questions with worked solutions, printable practice sheets and answer keys, practice exams built to the provincial exam structure, and exports for Kahoot, Blooket and Brightspace.

Coverage: Unit 1 From Kinetics to Equilibrium, Unit 2 Acids and Bases, Unit 3 Thermochemistry, Unit 4 Electrochemistry (the 36 content indicators), plus a small prior-learning review unit drawn from Chemistry 2202 (moles and molar mass, concentration and dilution, stoichiometry and yield, solubility and equilibrium basics). The Integrated Skills indicators are not included: they are assessed in labs and projects.

Exam presets: each unit has a practice unit test (25 marks: 11 selected response, 14 constructed response). The provincial exam review covers all four units and mirrors the provincial exam: 100 marks, 50 selected response and 50 constructed response, with the constructed response at cognitive levels 2 and 3 only. There is no separate mid-course preset: no mid-course structure for Chemistry 3202 could be verified from the course sources.

Part of the CDLI review apps collection. Status: draft, pending QA.
